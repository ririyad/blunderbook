# Petrov's queen trap

Trap · Petrov's Defence (C42) · you play Black. #traps

Copying White with 3... Nxe4 lets the queen come to e2, behind White's knight on e5. Retreating your knight opens the e-file: White's knight moves with a discovered check and attacks your queen at the same time.
