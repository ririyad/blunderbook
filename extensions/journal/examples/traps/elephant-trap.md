# Elephant trap

Trap · Queen's Gambit Declined (D51) · you play White. #traps

The knight on f6 is pinned to Black's queen, so the d5 pawn looks like it's lost. It isn't: after 6. Nxd5 Nxd5 7. Bxd8 Black checks with the bishop and wins your queen back, ending a piece up.
