# Caro-Kann smothered mate

Trap · Caro-Kann Defence (B17) · you play Black. #traps

White's queen on e2 lines up with your king on the e-file. Bringing the other knight to f6 shuts in your king: d7 is taken by your own knight, and the e-pawn can't take on d6. The knight jumps to d6 with mate.
