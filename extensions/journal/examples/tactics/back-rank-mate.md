# Back-rank mate

Tactic · you play White. #tactics

A king behind its own unmoved pawns has no square to run to. A rook or queen on the back rank mates it, unless something can block or take. Give your own king air (h3 or g3) before it happens to you.

```fen
6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/1q3PPP/3R2K1 w - - 0 1
White to move.
```
