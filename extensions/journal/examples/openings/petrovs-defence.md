# Petrov's Defence

Opening · C42 · you play Black. #openings

Instead of defending e5 you attack e4. If White takes on e5, chase the knight back with d6 first and only then take on e4: taking at once lets White's queen come to e2.

```pgn
[White "?"] [Black "?"] [ECO "C42"]
1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nf6 3. Nxe5 d6 4. Nf3 Nxe4 5. d4 d5 *
```

**Try it:** find the move in each position, then check the answer.

- After 2. Nf3: Play the next move of the opening. Answers: 2... Nf6 or 2... Nc6.
- After 3. Nxe5: White took on e5. Chase the knight first: don't take on e4 yet. Answer: 3... d6.
- After 4. Nf3: The knight has gone back. Now win your pawn back. Answer: 4... Nxe4.
