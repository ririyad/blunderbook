# Queen's Gambit Declined

Opening · D53 · you play White. #openings

You offer the c-pawn to pull Black's d-pawn away from the centre. Black declines with e6 and keeps d5. Your knight and bishop pressure d5 and the knight on f6; then you develop with e3 and Nf3 and castle.
