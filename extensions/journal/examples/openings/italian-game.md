# Italian Game

Opening · C50 · you play White. #openings

You take the centre with e4, attack the e5 pawn with the knight, and put the bishop on c4, where it aims at f7, the square next to Black's king that only the king defends. Then you castle and prepare d4 with c3.

**Try it:** find the move in each position, then check the answer.

- After 1... e5: Play the next move of the opening. Answer: 2. Nf3.
- After 2... Nc6: Develop the bishop to its Italian square. Answer: 3. Bc4.
- After 3... Bc5: Black has mirrored you with 3... Bc5. Play a calm move of the main line. Answers: 4. c3 or 4. O-O or 4. d3.
